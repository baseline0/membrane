# Research Strategy: 5-Year Vision for Malta

## Executive Summary

Malta evolves from a research simulator into a **publication-quality benchmarking framework** that funds quantum membrane computing research through short-term insurance consulting.

**Thesis:** Quantum Membrane Computing is an underfunded but theoretically rich niche. Malta positions you as the leading open-source research platform in that space by building credibility through published comparisons + real-world insurance applications.

---

## Year 1: Foundation (2025)

### Insurance Track
- **Goal:** Land first consulting engagement ($250k–$500k)
- **Deliverable:** POC for insurer's premium rating
- **Use Malta for:** FRPS rules + audit trails (publish case study)
- **Revenue:** Funds team for quantum research

### Research Track
- **Goal:** Publish first benchmark comparison
- **Deliverable:** "Membrane Evolutionary Algorithms: Empirical Analysis vs SOTA Meta-Heuristics"
- **Benchmarks:** CEC2017, TSPLIB (standard, non-quantum)
- **Conference:** IEEE CEC or GECCO (natural computing venues)
- **Malta feature additions:** Fuzzy membership, rule tracing, statistical harness

### Knowledge Base
- **Build:** `docs/quantum/research/sota.md` (30+ papers catalogued)
- **Obsidian notes:** Start `docs/quantum/notes/` with brainstorms on quantum angles

## Year 2: Quantum Foundations (2026)

### Insurance Track
- **Goal:** 2–3 additional client engagements
- **Use Learning:** Each client teaches you about FRPS decision trees in practice
- **Knowledge Feed:** Insights → `docs/quantum/notes/` on how fuzzy rules scale

### Research Track
- **Goal:** Publish Quantum-Inspired Membrane Algorithms (QMEAs) paper
- **Deliverable:** "Quantum-Inspired Fuzzy Reasoning P-Systems for Combinatorial Optimization"
- **Malta feature:** Quantum-bit representation + rotation gates (classical simulation)
- **Benchmarks:** CEC2017 + a combinatorial problem (knapsack, TSP variants)
- **Conference:** Journal of Membrane Computing or Natural Computing journal (peer-reviewed)

### Partnerships
- **Reach out:** 1–2 active researchers in quantum P-systems
  - Collaborate on benchmark design
  - Co-author second paper
  - Establish Malta as the "go-to open-source tool"

## Competitive Positioning

**Who else is here?**
- Academic papers (Păun, Ciobanu, etc.) — Theory-focused, no open-source tools
- Custom simulators (university labs) — Closed-source, not benchmarked against SOTA
- Gene-inspired / bio-computing startups (Ginkgo, Intrinsic) — Different focus (DNA computing, quantum HW)

**Malta's niche:**
- **Only open-source, production-grade benchmarking framework** for membrane computing
- **Published comparisons** showing when P-systems win (builds credibility)
- **Insurance credibility** (consulting clients + case studies)
- **Quantum bridge** (research → theory)
